Who I am.

I'm a multidisciplinary frontend developer with 15+ years of experience delivering websites and digital products across agency, e-commerce and content-driven environments. With a background in web design and UX, I bring a strong eye for detail to UI development, with a focus on clean, accessible and pixel-accurate implementation, animation and scalable component/design systems.

Having worked in agencies throughout my career, I'm comfortable working at pace, adapting quickly to new tools and technologies and collaborating across the full project lifecycle, from planning and technical strategy through to final delivery. I enjoy bridging the gap between design and development, working closely with designers, stakeholders and clients to turn ideas into polished user experiences.
